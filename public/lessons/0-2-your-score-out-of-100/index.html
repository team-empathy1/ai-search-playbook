Where you stand: your score out of 100

8 min read·Baseline

Before you fix anything, you need an honest number. This lesson gives you the exact scoring model we use with our own clients, so you know where you're starting from and can prove how far you've come.

The AEO Maturity Model

We score every store we work with out of 100, using a model built on the same four pillars as this playbook. Each pillar gets a level from 1 to 5, worth 5 points per level. So each pillar is worth up to 25 points, and the four together make 100.

  • Technical, can AI read you?
  • Content, do you answer the questions buyers ask?
  • Authority, do the engines trust you?
  • Measurement, can you see it working?

The five levels are the same for every pillar: Invisible (1), Emerging (2), Structured (3), Professional (4), and Leading (5).

The target

Certified AI-Ready is Professional on all four pillars. That's 80 out of 100, and it's the number this whole playbook is built to get you to.

What each level actually looks like

Here's the honest version, pillar by pillar. Score yourself as you read.

Technical. Invisible means AI crawlers are blocked and there's no schema. Emerging loads fine but has no structured data. Structured has core schema on key pages with gaps. Professional has valid schema, crawlers welcomed, and Core Web Vitals passing. Leading is rich, entity-linked schema monitored continuously.

Content. Invisible is product pages only, nothing an AI could quote. Emerging has a few posts that aren't answer-first. Structured has some real answers and an FAQ. Professional has a topic-cluster roadmap with cornerstone pieces AI actually quotes. Leading is a compounding engine that gets cited consistently.

Authority. Invisible has almost no off-site signals. Emerging has a handful of links, nothing managed. Structured has consistent core citations but it's ad hoc. Professional has quality backlinks, a clean profile, and strong reviews. Leading has earned press and genuine category authority.

Measurement. Invisible has no baseline and no tracking. Emerging has analytics installed that nobody looks at (this is most stores, honestly). Structured checks occasionally with no rhythm. Professional has a baseline plus a share-of-voice framework on a cadence. Leading has a live dashboard tied to action.

Score yourself now

Most Shopify stores we score for the first time land somewhere between 35 and 50. That's normal, and it's good news: the gap between where you are and 80 is exactly what this playbook closes, module by module.

Be harsh with yourself. A generous self-score just hides the wins you could be collecting. If you're straddling two levels on a pillar, take the lower one. The gating rule we use with clients: if any requirement of the higher level is missing, you're not there yet.

Write your four numbers down somewhere you'll keep. Your total is your baseline, and in Module 4 you'll re-score against it to prove the progress.

Do this now

Score each pillar 1 to 5, multiply each by 5, and add them up. Write down the four pillar scores and the total, plus today's date. That number is your baseline.
