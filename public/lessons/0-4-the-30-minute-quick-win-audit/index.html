The 30-minute quick-win audit

7 min read·Baseline

You've got your score and Claude's plugged in. Now run one fast sweep to catch the quick wins, the fixes that take minutes and start paying back straight away.

Why audit before you build

Most stores are leaking visibility in ways that take 10 minutes to fix and months to notice on their own. Blocked crawlers, empty collection pages, missing product identifiers, supplier descriptions copied onto 40 products. Finding them first means every module you work through lands on a clean foundation.

This audit is deliberately shallow and fast. The deep versions live in the modules. This is the sweep that tells you which modules will pay you back fastest.

The five checks

  1. Can AI get in? Open yourstore.com/robots.txt. Look for GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot or Google-Extended being disallowed. 85% of brands are accidentally blocking AI crawlers, and it's a two-minute fix. (Full fix in lesson 1.1)
  2. Do your products carry ID? Pick your three bestsellers and check for barcodes (GTINs) in Shopify admin. 60% of ecommerce catalogues have missing GTINs, and AI shopping surfaces lean on them hard. (Lesson 1.4)
  3. Are your collections empty? Open your five biggest collection pages. If they're a grid of products with no copy, you're sitting on your biggest untapped traffic pages. (Lesson 1.5)
  4. Would AI quote your content? Open your most recent blog post, if you have one. Does the first paragraph answer a question a buyer would actually ask? Or does it warm up for 200 words first? (Module 2)
  5. Does anyone vouch for you? Search your brand name plus "review" and your brand name on Reddit. What comes up is what the AI sees when it decides whether you're safe to recommend. (Module 3)

Turn it into a ranked plan

Score each check as a pass, a partial, or a fail. Fails on checks 1 and 2 are your first fixes: they're fast and they gate everything else. Fails on 3 and 4 tell you whether Module 1 or Module 2 is your biggest lever. A fail on 5 means Module 3 is where your gap is, and honestly, that's true for most stores.

Write the plan down as a list in priority order. As you work through the playbook, this is the list you're crossing off.

Do this now

Run the five checks. 30 minutes, tops. Write down your pass/partial/fail for each, then run the prompt below to have Claude turn it into your ranked action plan.

Run it with Claude 
Here are my 30-minute audit results for [yourstore.com]:
1. AI crawlers allowed: [pass/partial/fail + what you saw]
2. GTINs on bestsellers: [pass/partial/fail]
3. Collection pages have copy: [pass/partial/fail]
4. Content answers questions first: [pass/partial/fail]
5. Third-party proof (reviews/Reddit): [pass/partial/fail + what you found]

Turn this into a ranked action plan. For each item: the fix, why it matters for AI search visibility, effort (minutes/hours/days), and expected impact (high/medium/low). Order by impact-per-effort.
